# Internal review record

The `final_dimension_*` or `final_quotients_*` reports concern the exact merged manuscript hashes stated in those reports. `final_integration_checklist.txt` is the prospective mathematical checklist; the corresponding final reviews record its completion. Other reports retain their original version and source scope, including the v1 `final_format_diff_check.json` where present.

Review agents checked arithmetic, combinatorics, entropy/dimension, or quotient certificates as described in each report. Participation in earlier development is disclosed in the applicable report. These are internal AI-assisted reviews, not external peer review or proof-assistant verification.

Root's build, visual and package checks appear in `PUBLICATION_CHECKLIST.json`. The full-source reviews do not claim to certify publication mechanics or worldwide priority.

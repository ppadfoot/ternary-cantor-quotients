2. Counting proof: growing-parameter uniformity

The sampling lemma includes 0<delta<=1, including the singleton-set edge
case. Disjoint half-open arcs and the L2 derivative estimate give its
stated constant. The second and fourth moments are exact; addition of
two binary ternary words creates digits0,1,2 without carry, giving6^h
unnormalized additive energy.

Reduced fractions of denominator at most D and prime to3 are D^-2
separated. Sampling the second and fourth moments and interpolating gives
the third-moment exponent eta=log_3(27/16)<log_3 2. Multiplication by3^h
is a permutation of the same reduced-fraction set, which is essential:
Holder gives the unnormalized sum of pth powers with no extra factor
from its cardinality. Each fixed nonzero rational orbit is periodic and
avoids zero, so the fixed-D limit decays.

The divisor multiplicity bound uses intervals of diameter strictly less
than d and remains valid for d=1. The domain Y>=3 avoids the former small-Y
word-length ambiguity. On dyadic denominator bins, eta<s makes the bounds
summable; fixed-bin decay and a vanishing final partial bin establish the
average-discrepancy estimate in the stated growing Y,L range.

Good denominators are selected using common L=floor(log_3 X). The actual
witness count uses L_N=floor(log_3 NX); Fourier-modulus monotonicity
transfers goodness from L to L_N. Orthogonality then counts zero as well,
which is explicitly subtracted. The negative exponent s-1 is handled
in the correct direction when N is replaced by Y. The assumption
X^sY^(s-1)->infinity absorbs this subtraction uniformly.

3. Finite-word Fibonacci bound and valuation average

For r=1+3^e u, the displayed four-row carry table is exact. On admissible
paths its next carry depends only on B_q, not on which allowed x_q was
chosen. Induction therefore makes the offset at position i+e depend only
on the prefix before i and on x_i, even when intervening input digits
vary. At least one branch at i forces the later position, in the sense
of at most one continuation digit, without requiring the same digit
on all later prefixes.

The marking-tree comparison uses precisely this weaker forcing statement.
At a marked position there is one continuation; at an unmarked branching
position the two branches are bounded by deletion and neighbor-forcing
counts in the distance-e graph. Extra restrictions can safely be dropped.
Thus all locally admissible finite words, including dead ends, are bounded.
The path product gives phi^(n+e), also for e>=n.

Unit Cantor denominators have valuation e>=1 when distinct. Their residue
classes give pair counts O(Y^(2s) 2^-e). Summing phi^e over those classes
converges because phi<2. The injection M->NM then yields the complete
pair-overlap bound with the stated (XY)^g factor; no worst-case e bound
is substituted for the weighted average.

The choice alpha=(s-g)/(1+g)<1/6 meets the discrepancy condition. Choosing
small fixed c0 in Y=c0 X^alpha controls overlaps by half the first moment,
including the endpoint exponent beta. The nonoverlapping block Cantor
construction turns every positive integer witness into a positive-
Hausdorff-dimensional intersection. These steps prove both counting
inequalities, but no asymptotic or positive density statement.

4. Delayed carry model and positive-witness path bridge

M=621*3^(4k)-20 equals23*3^(4k+3)-20. For a putative finite witness,
the identity b+20a=23*3^m a gives the exact recurrence in the paper.
Its partial-sum formula proves integrality and eventual carry zero.
Divisibility by3 sharpens the raw numerator range[-34,31] to[-33,30],
so the 22-state carry interval[-11,10] is invariant.

The queue register is explicitly BINARY, not a ternary value. Flushing
the finite input gives a return to(0,0). Since M is1mod3, dividing out
the common power of3 makes a_0=b_0=1, so the first transition is to
(2^(m-1),7), not the initial state. At its first subsequent return,
a predecessor with oldest bit1 would need carry22or23, impossible.
The remaining possibilities are(0,0) and(0,-1); the first-return choice
rules out the former. Thus a positive witness must reach(0,-1).
A predecessor-closed set containing this seed but excluding the initial
state gives the claimed contradiction. No empty-path or positivity
loophole remains.

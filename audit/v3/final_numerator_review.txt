1. DEFINITIONS AND EXACT SOURCE QUESTION

Read ADRS arXiv2308.07252v1 directly, including Definition1.1 and the
catalogue introduction and 289 row on printed pages24-25.
https://arxiv.org/pdf/2308.07252v1

Their definition uses normalized Newman polynomials with constant term1;
universal means that the denominator divides the numerator as a polynomial.
For M congruent to1 modulo3, finite binary-ternary P,Q satisfying
P(3)=M Q(3) have the same lowest nonzero exponent. Cancelling their common
power of t gives exactly the normalized definition, and cancellation
does not change whether Q divides P. Monicity makes divisibility over
Q[t] equivalent to divisibility over Z[t]. The publication source's
normalization and usage are correct.

The cited catalogue concerns indecomposable representations and marks
the local type of289 with a question mark. The manuscript correctly fixes
the citation to arXiv version1 and proves the stronger all-representation
statement. It does not claim a classification for every integer or assert
that no later external literature has addressed this question.

2. CARRY GRAPH AND ALL-LENGTH INTERPRETATION

The transition 3c'=c+Ma-b yields the exact invariant
3^j c_j=M*sum_(i<j)a_i3^i-sum_(i<j)b_i3^i.
Nonnegativity is preserved: the only possible negative numerator is -1
from c=a=0,b=1, which is not divisible by3. The prefix bound gives
c_j<M/2 for every reachable carry. At M=289 the a priori range is
therefore the integers0 through144.

Enumerating the four pairs (a,b) recovers precisely the three residue
cases displayed in the source. The finite equality gives an integral
carry at every prefix by reduction modulo3^j. Padding both polynomials
with high zero digits gives a path from0 back to0. Conversely every such
path telescopes to the finite equality. There is no assumed witness-
length bound, and at least one input1 is equivalent to a positive witness.

The potential identity tH_c'=H_c+a h-b, multiplied by t^j and summed,
has telescoping left side t^n H_c_n-H_c_0. Both endpoint potentials
are zero for a returning path. Hence h Q-P=0 as a polynomial, not merely
after specialization at3. This proves the all-length theorem from a
finite coefficient-level certificate.

4. THE INFINITE FAMILY N_k=4*3^k-2

The review reconstructed both telescoping identities symbolically for
arbitrary k, rather than inferring them from numerical examples.

For Q_k, the jth exponent interval begins at(k+1)j and ends at kj+k-1.
Consecutive intervals are disjoint. The highest endpoint is k^2-1 and
the constant term is1. For k>=2, the P_k exponents from the first sum
are multiples of k; those from the second sum have residues1 through
k-2 modulo k. The final exponents n-1,n exceed every earlier one.
Therefore both polynomials have only0,1 coefficients and the asserted
degrees; no hidden coefficient2 occurs.

Shifting Q by k+1 and subtracting cancels successive blocks and leaves
the powers t^(ki), i=2,...,k+1, minus S_k. Independently,
(t-1)Q=sum_(i=1)^k t^(ki)-sum_(j=0)^(k-1)t^((k+1)j).
Multiplication by t^k-1 and the two finite geometric identities displayed
in the source yield the second telescoping identity after cancellation
of t-1 in Z[t]. Adding the identities and isolating the two endpoint
terms gives exactly
 P_k=(t^(k+1)+t^k-2)Q_k+R_k,
 R_k=3+2*sum_(i=1)^(k-1)t^i-t^k.

Here R_k(3)=0 but R_k is not the zero polynomial. For k>=2 its degree
k is strictly below k^2-1=deg Q_k. Thus polynomial division has a
nonzero remainder, proving that the specified representation is local.
This is stronger than showing that one proposed quotient polynomial
fails; the degree bound precludes any polynomial quotient.

The k=1 case is separately and correctly treated by Q_1=1 and
P_1=1+t^2, giving10. It is not claimed local. The k=2 boundary case
has Q_2=1+t+t^3, P_2=1+t^4+t^5+t^6 and R_2=3+2t-t^2; the empty
internal sum and the strict remainder-degree inequality both hold.
Supplemental independent exact polynomial checks for k=1 through25
confirmed both telescoping identities, the final decomposition and
the equality at3. These are regression checks supporting the symbolic
all-k argument, not its replacement.

5. SCOPE AND PUBLICATION FIT

The numerator theorem classifies every representation of289. The local
family theorem constructs one representation per N_k and correctly
does not classify all other representations of those integers or claim
minimal witness length. The numerator degree gives output length
k(k+1)+1=O((log N_k)^2). Because only O(log X) family members lie belowX,
this does not improve the global quotient-count exponent.

The section is a coherent arithmetic addition to a quotient paper: one
complete answer to the specifically cited catalogue question, one general
potential-certificate lemma used in that answer, and a complementary
explicit construction. Neither conclusion is presented as solving
Erdos406, excluding1 from E_2, or proving global counting asymptotics.

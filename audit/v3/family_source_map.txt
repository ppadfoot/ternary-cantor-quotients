RESULT AND SCOPE

For M_k = 621*3^(4k)-20, every k >= 0, construct a reachable carry
with two return words of lengths (m_k,L_k)=(3,12) for k=0 and
(4k+3,12k+6) for k>=1. Their first digits differ. The resulting compact
subsystem has exact Hausdorff dimension d_k, where
3^(-m_k*d_k)+3^(-L_k*d_k)=1. Hence dim_H C(1,M_k)>=d_k>0.
Combining this independently proved positivity with the retained
nonrepresentation theorem puts this specific family in H minus V.
The theta-root inequality is a consequence of L_k <= 4m_k.

No equality for the dimension of the full intersection is asserted.
No uniform positive lower bound, sharp quotient-count asymptotic,
or conclusion about the prescribed point 1 in E_2 is asserted.

RECONSTRUCTION IN THE PUBLICATION SOURCE

1. Integer path identity and digitwise carry lemma, including nonnegative
   intermediate carries and infinite 3-adic telescoping.
2. Three explicit paths at M=601, with all six word values.
3. The all-k reachable prefix P, its output polynomial q and the base81
   digit proof, separating k=1 from k>=2 and treating empty sums.
4. The two all-k return words, their exact values and output identities;
   the long output's base81 decomposition and the k=1 overlap to digit28.
5. Reachability from the actual initial state, not merely recurrence in an
   inaccessible component.
6. A complete all-depth mass-distribution proof for the prefix-free
   variable-length code and a stopping-cylinder upper cover. A fixed
   entry prefix preserves the subsystem dimension by similarity.
7. The root bound is used as the primary estimate, with the theta-bound
   as a consequence, rather than presenting only the weaker equal-length
   AB/BA construction from the development note.

printed page4, propose the family 621*3^(4k)-20 as sporadic nonquotients.

Lagarias, arXiv math/0512006v4, printed pages5-6, distinguishes finite
positive integer witnesses from positive-dimensional intersections and
already records M=52 as illustrating strict inclusion. Thus the global
strict-inclusion phenomenon and the existence of infinite families
obtained by multiplying an example by powers of3 are not claimed new.
https://arxiv.org/pdf/math/0512006v4

lower bound for this particular primitive sporadic family. This source map

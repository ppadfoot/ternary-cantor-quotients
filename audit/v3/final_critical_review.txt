1. NEW MATHEMATICAL ARGUMENTS

Section 10: For every k>=0, the entry path and two separated return words
are exhibited. I checked their lengths, integer values, carries and binary
ternary output expansions, including the exceptional k=0 and merged-digit
k=1 cases. The argument for k>=1 is symbolic and covers the whole parameter
range. Concatenation gives a valid 3-adic invariant subsystem after a fixed
entry prefix. The first digits of the return words differ; this supplies
the separation required by the variable-length construction. The cylinder
mass and stopping-word covering arguments prove that this subsystem has
the unique positive dimension d satisfying 3^(-m*d)+3^(-L*d)=1.

The article consistently restricts that exact formula to the constructed
subsystem. Its conclusion for the full intersection is the lower bound
by d, not an equality. The theta comparison follows from L<=4m and the
monotonicity of the defining equation. Together with the old exclusion
from finite quotients, this gives a family-specific positive-dimensional
intersection without a finite integer witness. It neither improves the
counting exponent nor establishes pointwise orbit finiteness.

Section 11: I independently reconstructed the 289 multiplication graph
from all 580 possible digit pairs on carries 0,...,144. It has 32 reachable
states and 42 edges. The 20 states with a return path to zero are separated
from a closed set of 12 nonreturn states. I derived the polynomial potentials
from shortest return paths and checked all 26 internal edge identities
coefficient by coefficient. The telescoping proof therefore applies to
arbitrary finite path length, including zero padding; it does not rest on
sampling a bounded set of representations. A nonzero explicit polynomial
product verifies that the assertion is nonvacuous.

The final TeX table itself was parsed and compared with the JSON certificate:
all 32 state rows, all 42 edges and all 20 polynomial potentials match.
Consequently the printed certificate is bound to the independently checked
graph. The general potential lemma and the specialization prove that every
finite representation of 289 is universal, not merely those tested by code.

For the explicit local family N_k=4*3^k-2, I independently checked the block-
sum identities and disjoint supports of P_k and Q_k. For k>=2 the nonzero
remainder R_k vanishes at 3 and has degree k<k^2-1=deg Q_k. This proves
nondivisibility in Q[t], while P_k(3)=N_k Q_k(3). The special k=1 case is
correctly identified as universal. The text makes no shortest-witness or
all-representations-local claim. Its N_k notation is distinct from the
sporadic family M_k of Section 10.

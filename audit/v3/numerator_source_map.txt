SCOPE
All finite representations of289 have the same polynomial quotient.
Only the displayed N_k representation is asserted local for k>=2.
There is no minimality claim, no claim that N_k has no universal
representation, no new overall counting exponent, and no pointwise406
conclusion. The graph/potential and finite-word methods use known carry
representations; the contribution is the explicit complete certificate
and the stated all-length consequences.
